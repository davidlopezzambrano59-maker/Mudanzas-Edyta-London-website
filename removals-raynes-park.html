Practical Moving Day Planning in Raynes Park

The following is practical moving day planning information — not legal advice and not a parking guide. It is knowledge that protects your move day from avoidable problems.

CPZ zones in Raynes Park SW20: the RPS zone covers core streets off Kingston Road including Dorien Road and Firstway — Monday to Friday 8:30am to 6:30pm, mixed permit holder and pay-and-display shared bays. The A1 zone covers Wimbledon Chase peripheral streets including Botsford Road — Monday to Friday 8:30am to 6:30pm, high enforcement for commuter spillover prevention. The RP zone immediately north and south of Raynes Park station — Monday to Saturday 8:30am to 6:30pm, extended Saturday controls for local shopping parades. The RPC zone covers cul-de-sacs and avenues off Coombe Lane — Monday to Friday 11:00am to 3:00pm only, part-day commuter deterrent. Free outside these core hours. On bank holidays, parking is entirely free across all Raynes Park CPZ zones on Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year's Day, Good Friday and all official UK bank holidays.

For narrow Apostles terrace streets, the seven working day parking bay suspension rule is essential — a Luton van will block through traffic without a formal suspension.
