How Much Does a 2-Bed Flat Move Cost in London? (And How Long Does It Actually Take?)

A standard 2-bed flat move in London typically takes between 4 and 8 hours from the first box loaded to the last item placed in your new home — though the exact time depends heavily on your floor level, lift access, parking distance, and how ready your belongings are on the day. Here's a full breakdown, based on real jobs we've completed, plus how our pricing works.

How Long Does Each Part of the Move Actually Take?

Based on real 2-bed flat jobs with our driver + 2 assistant crew using our Luton van, here's a realistic breakdown for loading time specifically (this doesn't include unloading — unloading is usually quicker, since taking items out of the van is faster than the careful positioning needed to pack it well):

  • Half a Luton van, ground floor, easy access: around 30 minutes to load
  • Full Luton van, ground floor, easy access: around 1 hour to load
  • Each additional floor with no lift: add roughly 30 minutes (half load) to 45 minutes–1 hour (full load) per floor
  • Buildings with a lift: loading typically takes 2 to 3 times longer than ground floor — especially in busier buildings where you're waiting for the lift to become free between trips
  • Long walking distances (van parked far from the entrance): we use a trolley, but depending on distance this can still roughly double the carrying time

On top of loading and unloading, add drive time between properties (London traffic and any roadworks can add significantly to this, especially crossing the city or during rush hour).

The Single Biggest Factor Most People Underestimate: Being Packed and Ready

Floor level and access matter, but how ready your belongings are on the day makes just as much difference — and it's the one factor that's entirely in your control.

When everything is boxed, taped, and ready to go, stacking the van is fast and efficient — boxes pack tightly, maximising space and often meaning everything fits in a single trip. When items are loosely packed or not packed at all, several things go wrong at once: the move takes longer, boxes and loose items can't be packed as efficiently in the van, and if you have a lot of belongings, it can mean two trips are needed instead of one.

We've seen this firsthand — on one same-day booking to Wimbledon, we got a driver and crew out quickly to help, but the customer had more belongings than she'd expected and was still packing when we arrived. Even with two of our crew pitching in to help pack, the move ended up running 1–2 hours longer than estimated.

If you'd rather not deal with packing yourself, we offer a full packing service — we bring boxes, tape, bubblewrap, packing paper, and protective film, and pack everything carefully the day before your move so it's ready to go straight into the van. Fragile items like TVs are wrapped in bubblewrap (ideally back in their original box) and positioned securely between mattresses in the van for extra protection during transit.

As with any move, we recommend fragile and valuable items are packed properly before moving day.

Example: A Full Packing, Move & Unpack Service

In February, we handled a complete packing, move, and unpacking service spread across three days for a 3-bed flat (living room, balcony, kitchen, and two bathrooms).

Day 1 (packing): A 4-man crew (driver + 3 assistants) spent 6 hours carefully packing and labelling every box by room — packing paper for kitchen items like glasses and plates, bubblewrap for fragile items like TVs and mirrors, protective film for chests of drawers and other less delicate items needing extra care, and mattress covers.
Result: The job filled 2 full Luton vans — a 4-man crew handled loading and unloading the following day.

As a rough guide, packing a 2-bed flat alone typically takes 4–6 hours with a driver + 2 assistants, or 3–4.5 hours with a driver + 3 assistants, depending on the size of the job. You can get a rough packing estimate through our calculator, but for the most accurate quote, it's best to send us a list of what needs packing so we can recommend the right crew size.

Real Example: A 2-Bed Flat Move from Elephant & Castle to West London

To give you a genuine sense of what a fuller 2-bed move looks like: we recently completed a move for a couple relocating from a 2-bed flat at Elephant & Castle to a residential house in West London, roughly a 1-hour drive apart.

The job ran from 9:45am to 4:45pm — a full day. Here's why:

  • The flat was a full Luton van's worth of belongings: 39 boxes, a sofa that needed disassembling, two wardrobes that had to be dismantled in half, bed frames, mattresses, and more
  • The pickup building was on the 10th floor with a lift — in a busy building, which meant repeated waiting between trips
  • Parking that had been planned in advance at the pickup address turned out to be too small (the customer had mentioned a garage, but it couldn't fit the van), causing a 30-minute delay while alternative parking was sorted

The lesson from this one: arranging parking permits and confirming access in advance — including checking van dimensions against any garage, gate, or archway — makes a real difference to how smoothly (and quickly) a move goes.

What Affects Your Move Time and Price

Floor level and lift access. Ground floor is always fastest. Stairs add time per floor; lifts help but often add more time than expected in busy buildings due to wait times between trips.

How packed and ready you are. As above — this is often a bigger factor than people expect.

Parking distance and access. Our Luton van needs realistic space to park close to the entrance. For reference, our van's dimensions are:

  • Overall length: 6.97m
  • Body width (excluding mirrors): 2.05–2.10m
  • Overall height: 2.83–3.00m
  • Cargo volume: 16–18 cubic metres

If you have a garage, gated entrance, or narrow access road, it's worth checking these dimensions against it in advance — this is exactly what caused the delay in the example above.

Furniture that needs dismantling or assembling. This varies a lot by item:

Item Typical time (dismantle + assemble)
Simple items (dining tables, desks, standard bed frames, chests of drawers) 30–45 minutes
Medium items (standard 2-door wardrobes) ~1 hour
Complex 3+ door wardrobes (e.g. flat-pack builds like IKEA PAX) 2–3 hours
Ottoman storage beds 1.5–2 hours

Older furniture with glued or extra-nailed joints, warped panels, or worn hardware tends to sit at the higher end of these ranges. For complex flat-pack wardrobes and older storage beds, we often bring an experienced handyman along, though our standard driver + 2 assistant crew can usually manage these too.

Time of day. Moves that start and finish between roughly 9am and 4pm generally benefit from lighter traffic compared to London's peak rush hour, especially for moves that cross the city.

Roadworks and street restrictions. These occasionally mean our drivers need to approach a property via a different route than planned, due to closures or height/width restrictions on certain streets.

Why We Don't Ask for an Inventory List

Most removals companies ask for a full room-by-room inventory, or require a home survey, before giving you a price. We price differently: by time and crew size rather than the volume of your belongings.

The reason this works is simple — most moves don't actually need all that detail. Most of the time, what people need is straightforward: the right crew and the right size van. That's why our standard 2-hour estimate works well for the majority of moves without any back-and-forth.

For more complex moves, a phone call or a more detailed conversation gives a much better estimate than any online calculator could. Here's why that matters: our Elephant & Castle example above was originally estimated at 5 hours, but ran longer than planned because the customer's declared inventory (15 boxes) turned out to be far less than what was actually there on the day (39 boxes), along with more furniture than expected — plus roadworks added around 20 minutes to the drive. For moves like this, declaring everything you have in advance is the single best way to avoid surprises on the day.

So the short version: get your instant estimate through the calculator for a typical move, and if your move is larger or more complex, get in touch directly so we can give you an accurate picture upfront.

How Our Pricing Works

All moves include a 2-hour minimum booking, even if the job takes less time. After the first two hours, you're only charged for the total time our team works on your move — from when loading begins at the pickup address until unloading is complete at the destination. We believe in transparent pricing — no hidden fees or surprise costs.

For a straightforward, well-packed 2-bed flat move with easy ground-floor or lift access, our standard 2-hour, 2-assistant estimate is often enough. For moves with multiple floors, no lift, long walking distances, unpacked belongings, or furniture that needs dismantling, these jobs typically run around 2–3 hours longer than the minimum — which is exactly why we bill fairly by actual time rather than a fixed guess.
