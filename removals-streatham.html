Parking and Access Planning in Streatham

Streatham's parking zones vary across SW16 and have been actively expanding since 2024. Knowing which zone covers your street before move day helps you plan ahead and avoid delays or fines. All parking arrangements are your responsibility to organise — the information below is provided so you can prepare in advance.

Zone M — Streatham Wells

Zone M covers the Streatham Wells area. Operational hours are Monday to Friday, 10am to 12noon, implemented in March 2025. Part-day restriction — outside these hours parking is generally unrestricted on zoned streets.

Zone I — St Leonard's North

Zone I covers St Leonard's North with controlled hours Monday to Friday, 12noon to 2pm. The zone covers streets bounded by Prentis Road, Streatham High Road, Drewstead Road and Garrad's Road. Pay by Phone applies with a 1 hour maximum stay and no return within 1 hour. If your property falls in Zone I, you will need to plan loading within a tight window or apply for a parking bay suspension in advance.

Zone O — St Leonard's Central

Zone O covers St Leonard's Central with controlled hours Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 6:30pm — the widest hours zone in Streatham. Covers streets bounded by Streatham High Road, Tooting Bec Gardens, the Tooting-to-Streatham railway line and the Wandsworth borough boundary. Pay by Phone with 4 hour maximum stay and no return within 1 hour. If your property falls within this zone you will need to plan your loading carefully.

Zone U — Stanthorpe Triangle

Zone U covers the Stanthorpe Triangle with controlled hours Monday to Friday, 12noon to 2pm. The zone covers streets bounded by Streatham High Road, Shrubbery Road, Gleneldon Road and the Streatham-to-Tulse Hill railway line. These are the tight-access streets off the High Road where van positioning matters — check your zone before you assume unrestricted parking on move day.

Streatham Hill East CPZ Extension

Implemented in January 2025, the Streatham Hill East CPZ Extension covers Adare Walk, Hitherfield Road, Keymer Road, Leigham Court Road between the junction of Streatham High Road and Knollys Road, Leigham Vale, Mountearl Gardens, Mount Nod Road, Romeyn Road, Rosedene Avenue, Stockfield Road and Streatham Close. If your property sits on any of these streets, you should check current controlled hours and payment requirements with Lambeth Council before your move date — this extension is recent and many residents are still adjusting to the new restrictions.

Parking bay suspension

If you need to reserve a space for a removal van on a restricted street you can apply to Lambeth Council for a formal parking bay suspension. Apply at least 7 working days in advance. Contact Lambeth Council's parking team on 020 7926 6799 or email cpzconsultation@lambeth.gov.uk to confirm current requirements and fees for your specific street.

Single yellow line loading

You can load from a single yellow line for a maximum of 40 minutes. Civil Enforcement Officers will issue a Penalty Charge Notice if the vehicle is unattended for more than 5 consecutive minutes. Check for kerb blips — vertical yellow markings on the kerbstone indicate an absolute loading ban regardless of the single yellow line exemption.

School streets

Hitherfield Primary School on Hazel Road and Sunnyhill Primary School on Sunnyhill Road operate active school streets with ANPR enforcement during term time. Hours are typically 8:15am to 9:15am and 2:45pm to 3:45pm, Monday to Friday. No exemptions for removal vans. If your move involves roads near these schools plan your arrival outside these windows.
