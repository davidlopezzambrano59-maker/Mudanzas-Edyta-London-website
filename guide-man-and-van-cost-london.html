How Much Do Removals Cost Compared to a Man and Van?

If you're weighing up how much do removals cost for a full-service move versus a man and van, the difference usually comes down to what you're moving and how much help you need. A man and van setup — a driver, a Luton van, and however many assistants you choose — is typically the better fit for smaller jobs: studio and 1-bed flats, single-room moves, student moves, or anyone who's largely packed and organised already. A full removals crew with more assistants and a bigger van tends to make more sense for larger, multi-room homes.

Average cost of a removal company in the UK varies a lot by job size and location, but London generally sits at the higher end due to traffic, parking restrictions, and demand — which is exactly why booking a crew size that matches your actual job matters for keeping removal costs UK-wide manageable.

What Affects Man and Van Cost in London Specifically

Distance and mileage. At £1.50/mile, longer cross-London jobs add up — but so does time stuck in traffic, which is often the bigger cost driver on longer routes.

Congestion Charge. If either your pickup or dropoff address falls within the Congestion Charge zone, this is added to your invoice and always communicated to you upfront — no surprises. ULEZ doesn't add any extra cost, since our vehicles are compliant.

Parking access. A van that can park right outside costs you less time (and therefore less money) than one that has to park further away — every extra bit of walking distance adds to the job.

Floor level and lift access. Ground floor is fastest. Stairs and busy lifts both add real time to loading and unloading — but we don't charge extra just for floors or stairs; you're only billed for the actual time the job takes.

Extra stops. If you need an additional stop between your pickup and dropoff (for example, dropping something off on the way), this adds a flat £10 per extra stop — no vague surcharge, just a clear, fixed amount.

How packed and ready you are. This is one of the biggest levers in your control — see our full 2-bed flat move cost & timing guide for a detailed breakdown of how packing readiness affects job length.

Real Man and Van Jobs: What They Actually Look Like

To show real man and van cost in action rather than just theory, here are three genuine jobs from the last couple of weeks.

A student move in central London (3 hours). Not a large job — not many items — but the student had requested our packing service and hadn't packed beforehand, so the crew used 15-20 boxes and some bubblewrap for fragile items, which took up the bulk of the time. Two other factors extended the job: navigating parking at both ends in central London (planned in advance — the crew used a backstreet at the pickup building rather than the main road, which was red-route and no-stopping only), and the drop-off itself, which was student accommodation on the second floor, involving both walking distance and stairs. Since the job was in central London, the Congestion Charge was included on the invoice.

A short local move in Streatham (just under 2 hours). A handful of furniture items, straightforward job — the driver helped with loading throughout, since it was a small enough load for him to assist directly.

A single-item move from Colliers Wood to Morden. About as simple as a man and van job gets — one item, short local distance — and again, the driver helped load it himself.

For small moves like these — single items, a few pieces of furniture, or short local jobs — our driver is always happy to help load, often at no extra cost, since you're not paying for a full assistant you don't need. Where it gets more involved (multiple floors, or more items than expected — which happens sometimes, since not every customer has a fully clear picture of what they're moving until the day) booking 1 assistant is highly recommended, unless you're happy to load the van yourself.

As a general rule: single-item moves, student moves, short local jobs, and simple 1-bed moves usually fall comfortably within our 2-hour minimum — which is exactly why our instant quote calculator works so well for this kind of job.
